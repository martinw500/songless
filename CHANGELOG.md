# Changelog

User-facing Songless changes are recorded here. Internal commits and media-maintenance batches are not treated as releases.

## Unreleased

- Continue reviewing catalogue metadata, artwork, audio openings, and difficulty outliers.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-08-30

### Added

- 804-song hosted catalogue across five difficulty levels.
- Multi-select era and broad-genre filters.
- Persistent no-repeat cycles scoped to each selected mix.
- Intro and main-hook modes, live volume control, and optional auto reroll.
- Phone-safe clue playback, responsive layouts, feedback, and result controls.

### Changed

- Difficulty uses intro recognition, broad reach, current relevance, and longevity instead of random allocation.
- Complete result playback restarts from game-time zero.

### Fixed

- Incorrect or duplicated recordings, featured-artist credits, album artwork, quiet openings, and stale artwork across rerolls.
